# Crossing, streetscape and rotary polish

## Implemented

- Commercial signs consolidated into larger, simpler panels; advertiser variants are unique per building.
- Rounded vehicle bodies and twin, softly fading road headlight footprints.
- Supporting pedestrians patrol existing non-crossing sidewalk routes and turn around at their endpoints.
- Vehicle queues stage outside the complete crossing polygon. Pedestrian WALK waits for physical vehicle clearance, not just a timer.
- Connected egress routes keep admitted vehicles moving toward an outlet.
- HIGH streetscape adds 8 functioning signal structures, 16 lamps and 4 trees. Station-front additions separately validate road, ramp, pedestrian-flow, walkway, solid and existing-station-fixture clearance.
- Street lamps brighten at night, with a single instanced glare batch and no additional point/spot lights.
- Four full-sized rotary buses use a 187.25m mapped route, stop for 18 seconds, follow one another, yield at the crossing approach and return through the off-centre entry/exit.
- Supplemental fixture placement and bus swept-path data are precomputed. Regenerate with `npm run bake:polish` after changing the static map or placement rules.

## Remaining differences

This is not a pixel-identical reproduction of the reference. Pedestrian silhouettes/spacing, detailed advertisement artwork, broad-plaza landscaping and the overall day-light colour balance still differ. Bus service is a visual simulation, not an actual timetable. The old unrelated evidence files were left outside this commit.
