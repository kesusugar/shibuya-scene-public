Known limitations / design decisions:
- Full S6 furniture ownership area X[-65,180], Z[5,205] excludes new S8 furniture. Hachiko fixtures remain S6-owned; S8 station-region counts refer only to points outside this rectangle.
- Street equipment is a deterministic POC approximation along S2 sidewalk boundaries, not surveyed fixture locations. OSM trees take priority with <3.5m recorded adjustment; remaining vegetation is supplemental.
- Signals use nearest road travel tangent; missing OSM forward/backward direction is explicitly marked as an assumption. No signal control state machine. Signal count stays constant across tiers for major infrastructure.
- Placement protects full furniture/tree crown envelopes and reserves an inland 1.2m strip. Building frontage midpoint is an approximate entrance clearance; exact door positions are not mapped.
- S8 clipping uses coordinates rounded to 1 micrometre to avoid polygon-clipping ring failures; large pedestrian paths are indexed as separate buffered segments. No S2 geometry changed.
- Shared model envelopes include up to 1cm surface overlays (cabinet seam / vending atlas face); these are intentional fixture construction offsets.
- CPU model construction is synchronous and may pause startup or quality switching for tens of seconds. No FPS or responsiveness guarantee inferred from bounded draw batches.
- Normal scene has no S8 debug geometry. Debug shows category bounds, normals, rejected candidates, region density stems, cable geometry and S6 exclusion.
- Scene totals are unchanged S7 CPU totals plus S8, excluding S0 grid/axes and S1 debug lines. They are not measured GPU draw calls.
