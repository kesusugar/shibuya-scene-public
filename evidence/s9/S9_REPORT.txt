Architecture / limitations:
- Directed surface-road graph follows OSM one-way metadata. Shared OSM coordinate vertices define connectivity; grade-separated and prohibited roads excluded. Geometry stays within the existing 500m tile.
- Left offsets use width/estimated lanes. No lane changes or immediate U-turns. Dead ends and unsafe continuations use pooled despawn/recovery rather than reversing through traffic.
- Curved connections are sampled with each vehicle envelope and a 30cm static margin. Next lanes without a safe waiting position are excluded per vehicle type. This is a conservative route network, not a complete surveyed traffic model.
- Only roadside original procedural vehicle meshes are added. Seven types; taxi roof marker, sedan/kei/van/bus/kei-truck/scooter proportions. No manufacturer logos or copied assets.
- Fixed 82-slot pool, 30Hz simulation, reusable spatial buckets and scalar oriented-box tests. Following looks 25m ahead; junction exit checks extend to at most 60m. Stop-line queues and junction reservation cells prevent enter-then-stop behavior.
- Signal cycle is a POC NS / yellow / clearance / EW / yellow / clearance / future pedestrian phase. Signal groups cluster mapped crossings; S8 lens overlays share one instanced mesh. S10 read API is exposed through signalAPI; no pedestrians are generated.
- Permission is acquired before entry and retained while a vehicle clears the same crossing group, including a legal turn. Red violations count entry without a permit; vehicles already clearing a permitted movement may finish. WALK is withheld until the group is empty.
- Admission and permit-yield counters count retry evaluations, not distinct vehicles. crossingEntries records actual front-bumper entries. A stopped vehicle outside crossing/junction space yields its permit so another axis can flow.
- Reservations persist until the rear clears the connection. Entry is blocked if exit space is occupied. Tight/ambiguous routes are excluded; dead-end and >100s stuck recovery counts above are explicit limitations.
- Parked vehicles occupy selected non-major curb lanes, outside scramble and S6 station exclusion; they never occupy a sidewalk or crossing approach. Other traffic safely queues behind parked vehicles; no overtaking/lane changing in S9.
- Geometry safety is CPU sampled and polygon bounded. Continuous real-world traffic engineering, actual signal timings, OSM turn-restriction relations and rigid-body physics are outside this POC.
- S0-S8 runtime modules remain unchanged. Historical S7/S8 forward-stage guards now permit the newly authorized buildTraffic; all earlier assertions remain. Existing untracked validation logs are untouched.
- S8 road-contained fixtures and S9 road-contained vehicle envelopes are disjoint; explicit S8 fixture, sign and station volumes are included when building the integrated graph.
- Scene totals are sums of CPU geometry, not frustum-culling/GPU draw calls. Renderer resolution and profiles are unchanged. WebGL/FPS claims require actual browser support.
